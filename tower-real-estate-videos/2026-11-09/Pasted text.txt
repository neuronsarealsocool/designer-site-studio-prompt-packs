# Choose your floor: recreation prompt

Build a single-file, full-screen hero for a luxury residential tower, exactly as described below. It is one page with three states: (1) a dawn scene of a slender glass tower beside a misty lake, with a dark tinted-glass "Choose your floor" panel on the right, where hovering a floor lights up that floor's real windows; (2) a cinematic fly-in, made from two AI video clips, that climbs the tower to its curved balconies and passes through the glass into the apartment's living room; (3) two room walk-throughs: tags in the living room walk the camera through the pivot door into the bathroom, or through the left doorway into the bedroom, and back. Everything that moves inside the scene (mist, the camera flights) is video. Code only animates the interface. The interface is deliberately minimal: tinted glass with no borders, no shadows, no white panels and no washes over the imagery, apart from a light darkening at the screen sides for text contrast. Use plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with no frameworks and no build step.

## 2. Fonts

Two variable Google Fonts, latin subset, `font-display: swap`, both preloaded.
- **Outfit**, variable 100–900 (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit): the headline (300), the level numeral (200), the floor tag number (300) and the interior titles (250).
- **Figtree**, variable 300–900 (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree): all body copy (500), labels (600), links and tags (500).
Load from Google Fonts (`family=Outfit:wght@100..900&family=Figtree:wght@300..900`) or self-host as WOFF2. Antialiasing: `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`.

## 3. Head and colour tokens

- Title "Choose your floor". Meta description "Point at a floor of the tower and its rooms light up. Slide to a level, press enter, and fly in through the glass." Viewport width=device-width, initial-scale=1. Preconnect to images.higgs.ai and d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net.
- Tokens: white #ffffff (all text); white-soft rgba(255,255,255,.88) (descriptions, slider ends); glass rgba(58,48,86,.26) (the panel tint); numeral #f5e6a6 (the thin cream level number); knob #f2cf5b; enter-bg rgba(250,238,178,.82); enter-ink rgba(120,110,72,.75); tag-glass rgba(40,34,58,.42) (room tags); page background #4b4068 (only visible before images load); interior solid background #2e2840.
- Register `@property --fy { syntax: "<number>"; inherits: true; initial-value: 500 }`. It holds the top edge of the selected floor in frame pixels and can transition smoothly.

## 4. Scaling system

The scene is designed on one 1440×900 frame.
- **Cover unit** `--u = max(100vw/1440, 100vh/900)`. The frame is 1440u × 900u, fills the viewport and crops the overflow. It is centred horizontally (left 50%, translateX(−50%)); its top sits at `(100vh − 900u) × 0.12`, so wide, short screens lose only a little of the sky and never the tower's crown.
- **Contain unit** `--c = min(100vw/1440, 100vh/900)`. All UI is sized in c so it never leaves the viewport.

## 7. Interactions and the fly-in

- **Hover the tower**: on pointermove over .tower, add `on` to .zoom and set the level from the pointer. Setting a level sets --fy (the lit strip and tag glide there), the tag number, the knob and range value, the numeral (which fades from .25 to 1 over 260 ms ease-out when it changes) and the type and description. On pointerleave, remove `on` unless the range input has focus.
- **Slider**: input sets the level; focus turns the highlight on; blur turns it off; the Enter key inside the slider enters.
- **Enter** (click the tower, which first sets the level under the pointer, or click ENTER):
  1. Mark the mode inside and the room living. Queue the fly and in clips as urgent, preload and decode the living still, and queue the room clips (section 9).
  2. Set the panel to opacity 0 (its .25 s transition) and pointer-events none. Set the back link to "Back to the tower" and show the bar. Show .inside (`live`) with the still at opacity 0.
  3. Set .zoom's transform-origin to 47.8% horizontally and ((fy + 11)/900 × 100)% vertically, the selected floor. Animate scale 1 → 1.12 over 420 ms, cubic-bezier(.5,0,.3,1), filling forwards, while the clip starts.
  4. Wait for the fly clip (at most 2.5 s). Play it with speed profile (2.2, 3.6, 3.0), fading it in over 220 ms. 240 ms after it starts, add `solid` to .inside.
  5. When it ends, show the in clip at full opacity, hide the fly clip and play the in clip with profile (3.0, 3.4, 1.7). The fly clip's last frame is the in clip's first frame.
  6. Settle on the living room: wait for the still to decode (at most 5 s), set it on .still at opacity 1, wait two animation frames, then hide and pause every clip. Cancel the zoom animation, remove `on` and pause the scene loop.
  7. Show the copy: fill it, then fade the copy up from 14px below over 700 ms after a 250 ms delay (cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)) and fade the tags in over 500 ms after a 450 ms delay.
  If the fly clip is not ready in time, settle immediately and animate the still from scale 1.12 / opacity 0 to rest over 900 ms.
- **A room tag**: fade the copy and tags out over 200 ms. If currently in a room, walk out first (its walk-out clip, settling on the living room). Then walk in: that room's clip (urgent, waiting up to 15 s), set to full opacity (its first frame equals the living still), played with profile (2.4, 3.6, 1.7), then settle on the room still. Immediately queue that room's walk-out clip as urgent. Show the copy.
- **Back to the living room**: the same walk, using the walk-out clip, ending on the living room.
- **Back to the tower**: hide the copy and the bar, restart the scene loop, remove `solid`, animate .zoom from scale 2.2 to 1 from the floor's origin over 950 ms (cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)) while the still fades out over 450 ms. Then hide .inside and restore the panel (opacity 1, pointer-events back).
- **Register interest** is a plain link; prevent its default.
- Keyboard: remember whether the last input was a key or a pointer, and move focus to Back / ENTER after transitions only for keyboard users. Escape inside triggers the Back button.

## 9. Video playback details

- **Speed curve**: each clip plays with a profile (start, cruise, finish). With p = currentTime / duration: for p < .25 the rate is start + (cruise − start) × s(p/.25); for p > .62 it is cruise + (finish − cruise) × s((p − .62)/.38); otherwise cruise, with s(x) = x²(3 − 2x). It is updated every animation frame, so each 5 s clip takes about 1.7–1.8 s and settles softly. Guard against a missing `ended` event with a timeout of duration / min(start, finish) + 1.5 s.
- **In-memory clips**: fetch each MP4 whole (mode cors), make a blob URL and use it as the src; fall back to the direct URL on failure. A queue runs at most two fetches at once, in order of need: fly and in first (when the page goes idle), then the walk-in and walk-out clips. Queuing an urgent clip moves it to the front. A clip is ready at readyState 4.
- **Images**: decode each still ahead (decoding async, `decode()`, keep a reference) and only swap it onto .still after it has decoded.
- **Scene loop**: set its src at start-up and play; pause on reaching the living room; restart on Back to the tower.

## 11. Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every transition and animation is off, the scene loop never plays, no entrance animation runs and no clips play: Enter cuts to the living room, tags cut to the room, Back cuts back.

## 12. Acceptance checklist

- At 1440×900 the panel spans roughly x 956–1420, y 50–830, as dark tinted glass with no border or shadow; the tower occupies roughly x 600–775.
- Hovering lights only the hovered floor's windows inside the tower outline; the "— N" tag, numeral, knob, type and description follow, with the exact copy and prices above.
- Enter fades the panel, shows "Back to the tower" and "Register interest •", then flies up the tower to the balconies and in through the glass in about 3.5 s, landing exactly on the living still. The tower never shows through while inside.
