# **Intended carousel behaviour**

The design must visually support this interaction:

When the user clicks an arrow, taps a product, uses the keyboard, or swipes:

1. All four visible option items move one position along the same curved path.  
2. The incoming product moves toward the central cup.  
3. The product travels behind the cup.  
4. While it is hidden behind the cup, the large cup image crossfades to the newly selected drink.  
5. The background/accent state, product title, price, selected variation, button value, and product counter update together.  
6. The newly selected drink occupies the central position.  
7. Its raw-ingredient marker disappears from the surrounding options because the central cup now represents it.  
8. The previously selected drink returns to the carousel as its corresponding raw ingredient.

The still image should communicate that this curved path and behind-the-cup transition are physically plausible.
