# AER HOUSE — a private jet holding level in dark cloud, five staged camera moves

Output `index.html`, `css/style.css`, `js/scene.js` and `js/main.js` (ES modules). Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS plus three.js `0.160.0` from jsDelivr through an import map (`three` → `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.0/build/three.module.js`, `three/addons/` → `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.0/examples/jsm/`). Use the models and videos below exactly. Do not redesign, simplify, omit sections or invent replacement assets. Do not split the page into framework components or put the scene in an iframe.

## 1. Concept
A fictional private aviation house, Aer House, on a blue-black page. The hero is one full-screen WebGL scene: a pearl-white business jet flies straight and level through charcoal cloud that streams past it. The jet barely moves (a few centimetres of float, a fine vibration); the camera does the work. Five deliberate scroll gestures each play one complete 2.6-second camera move between six fixed views, and the camera holds each view until the next gesture. After the fifth view the page scrolls on to a pinned fleet reel, a charter film that opens to full width, a floating ownership carousel on an arc, a contact form over drifting cloud and a footer. Sound is synthesised in the browser and stays off until the visitor turns it on.

## 4. The flight scene (js/scene.js, shared by the hero and the charter top view)
- Renderer: antialias, pixel ratio min(dpr, 2), ACES filmic tone mapping, sRGB output, `localClippingEnabled`.
- Scene: background `#05070a`, `FogExp2(#05070a, .03)`. Environment: a PMREM of a near-black room (sphere `#07090c`) with four long soft panels (top `#dfe8f2`, cool sides `#6f86a3`/`#3b4a5e`, floor `#1a1f26`). Lights: Hemisphere(`#9fb4cc`, `#05070a`, .35), key Directional(`#fff6ec`, 2.2) at (−6, 10, −4), rim Directional(`#9fbadb`, .7) at (8, 2, 10).
- Jet: load, centre on its bounding box, rotate −90° about Y so the nose points −Z, scale so the longest side is 10 units. Replace each material with MeshPhysicalMaterial keeping the model's `map` (it carries the painted windows) and `normalMap`, `fog:false`, clearcoatRoughness .08. Finishes: pearl `#e9ecef` metal .35 rough .32 clearcoat 1; obsidian `#1a1d23` metal .6 rough .22 env 2.4; silver `#b9bec6` metal .9 rough .26; navy `#16223a` metal .5 rough .3; bronze `#9a7a5a` metal .85 rough .3.
- Level flight: y = .06·sin(2πt/6) + .025·sin(2πt/4); roll .006·sin(2πt/12) + .0008·sin(2πt/.25); pitch .004·sin(2πt/6).
- Clouds: 150 sprites around a clear tunnel (|x| from 7 to 35) plus 70 wide sprites in a lower deck at y −9 to −15, all inside a 160-unit box along Z. Each frame z = −120 + ((baseZ + 16t) mod 160), so cloud streams past at 16 units/s. Opacity fades in over the first and last 12% of the box (no visible reset) and fades to 0 within ~3 units of the camera. Brightness = tint `#5d6672` × (.05 + 1.5·exp(−distance²/1400 from the jet)), so only cloud near the jet is lit. Sprite textures: five 256px canvases of 5-octave value noise inside a soft ellipse, lighter on top, alpha = d^1.4 × .9.

## 5. Hero (100svh, overflow hidden)

- Six views in the jet's frame (`pos` → `look at`), with a `via` control point for each move so paths arc around the aircraft (QuadraticBezierCurve3, ease-in-out cubic, 2.6 s; targets lerp with the same ease):
  0. Front three-quarter: (5.6, .3, −12.5) → (3.4, −.5, −1)
  1. Side profile: (17, .6, .5) → (0, .1, 0), via (15, 1.4, −9)
  2. Elevated three-quarter: (10, 9.5, 5) → (0, −.4, −.5), via (16, 6, 3)
  3. Cockpit and forward cabin: (3.1, 1, −7.4) → (0, .3, −3.4), via (8, 5, −6)
  4. Engine and wing: (5.6, 2.2, 6.8) → (1, .2, 1.2), via (5.6, 2.4, −1)
  5. Rear three-quarter, wide: (−12, 3.6, 17) → (0, 0, −2.5), via (1.5, 4.8, 14)
- Portrait screens multiply each camera's distance from its target by min(2.2, 1.3/aspect).
- Input: a gesture is the first wheel event after 220 ms of no wheel events, so trackpad momentum never counts twice. While the page is at the top and a move is playing, every wheel event is cancelled. Down at stage 5 (settled, fresh gesture) is not cancelled: the page scrolls on. Up at stage 0 is ignored. Touch: cancel touchmove while the hero owns the gesture; on touchend a 40px swipe moves one stage. Keys: ArrowDown/PageDown/Space forward, ArrowUp/PageUp back. "Explore the Jet" plays move 1. Returning to the top restores hero control at the current stage.
- Copy: kicker "Aer House · Private aviation"; H1 "Quiet, above / *everything.*" (clamp(52px, 8.4vw, 132px)); lede "A small fleet of private jets for people who would rather arrive rested than early."; button "Explore the Jet ↓". ## 7. Sound (Web Audio, created on the first click of a Sound toggle)
Master gain eases to .5. Pad: 55/82.4/110.6 Hz oscillators through a 260 Hz low-pass with slow LFOs. Wind: looping noise through a 520 Hz band-pass. Engine hum: noise through a 140 Hz low-pass plus a faint 2960 Hz whine. Each camera move plays a whoosh (band-pass sweeping 260 → 1500 → 320 Hz over the move). Card hover plays a short two-tone chime, throttled to one per 140 ms.

## 8. Video and accessibility
Videos are `muted loop playsinline preload="none"` with posters, and only the active card ±1, the visible film and the contact background play. A Motion toggle (defaulting to `prefers-reduced-motion`) replaces camera moves with a quick fade, stops the jet's float and pauses videos. Visible focus rings, 44px targets, alt text and `aria-live` captions.

Scrolling back to the top and up reverses one view per gesture. The jet never banks or drifts.
